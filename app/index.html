    Roadmap to the platinum

    PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 difficulty and about 200 hours, one playthrough, all offline — no online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission medals. There is free roam after the story but no mission replay, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter (15 manual slots).

    1. Step 1 · Play the story 60–80 hours

      Finish all five chapters at your own pace. Story trophies pop automatically — 12 chapter and mission trophies plus the gold for the final mission. Along the way: do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude (see the missable warning below), start the side-character questlines as they appear (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret), and scan vehicles and study animals whenever you see new ones — that progress counts toward 100% later.

      Earns: the 18 story trophies, plus whatever activities you try on the way.

    2. Step 2 · The 100% Completion checklist about 100 hours

      After the credits the checklist unlocks (Touch Pad, then Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion). It is lenient — roughly 60% of true completion: any 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 15 of 18 card sets, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations and all 15 Target Decks. Nothing on it is missable. This step also earns A Long Road (gold), Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Don't Touch My Deck, One Last Thing, Maximum Withdrawal and most of the money and robbery trophies.

      Earns: A Long Road and most of the grind trophies.

    3. Step 3 · Strangers & Freaks and cleanup about 10 hours

      Finish the 8 stranger mission strands and mop up whatever is left — the four trophies with no confirmed method yet (In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) are the ones to check the linked guides for. When the last trophy pops, the platinum follows immediately.

      Earns: Strangers & Freaks and the platinum.

    ⚠ The one possibly missable trophy

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida. During Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia talks about meditating in the kitchen in the morning and walks to the balcony at Jason's house. Follow her and choose Accept. That session may be required (still unconfirmed) and it is gone after Chapter 1. Do it when it appears. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story.

    Cheats switch trophies off

    Turning on a cheat disables trophies and saving. Cheats only unlock after the story anyway — keep them off for the whole trophy run.
